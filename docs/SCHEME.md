# Q1 FY27 Scheme — East (permanent reference)

> Source of truth, preserved for the life of the scheme. Machine-readable copy lives in
> [`data/east/gifts.csv`](../data/east/gifts.csv); the original workbook is
> [`docs/Q1_scheme_East.xlsx`](Q1_scheme_East.xlsx). North & Central is documented at the
> bottom of this file.

## Mechanics
- **Region:** East · **50 points per MT** · gift value reference **₹12 / point**.
- **Qualifying minimum:** **12 MT** total. Below 12 MT → not qualified.
- **Points:** linear — `MT × 50` (used on the dashboard).
- **Early-bird bonus:** **×1.25** on volume billed **on/before 20 May 2026**, when that
  pre-20-May volume is itself ≥ 12 MT.
